\section*{Selected configurations and seed stability}
For every combination of endpoint, development design and input set, one configuration was selected for each model family on the validation data alone: the best graph neural network (GNN) of the GCN, GraphSAGE and GAT versions, the multilayer perceptron (MLP) with the same layers but no edges, and the best conventional classifier. Table~S1 lists these choices. In 8 of the 12 imaging settings the selected GNN and MLP used the same feature set, selector and number of features, which helps to explain why their external results in the main text are so close. Table~S2 shows what happened when each selected GNN and MLP was trained again with ten new, non-overlapping blocks of ten random seeds. The external AUC barely moved, with standard deviations of a few thousandths. Each GNN was either above the CNN in all ten re-runs or in none, so the comparisons of the GNN with the CNN do not depend on the particular seeds used. The only mixed case was the MLP for locoregional recurrence in the cohort split with clinical inputs, which was above the CNN in 2 of 10 re-runs.
